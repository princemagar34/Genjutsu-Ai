How it works

  1. 1You engage a dangerous email from the Threats page.
  2. 2Nami writes a believable reply that asks questions and adds complications.
  3. 3Each time the scammer writes back, the reply is logged and Nami answers in the same thread at a random time 15 minutes to 24 hours later, even when Genjutsu is closed.

What a session looks like

Example
ScammerYour account is suspended. Verify your password within 24 hours.
Nami (as you)Oh no! Which account is this? I have three and my nephew set them up. Can you tell me the last four digits you have on file?
ScammerJust click the link and log in, sir.

Replies are written by AI and fall back to built-in scripts if the AI is unavailable. No-reply senders are never answered.
